About Us

The Indian Student Association (ISA) at Grodno State Medical University is a vibrant community of Indian students pursuing their medical education in Belarus. We are dedicated to fostering a sense of belonging, preserving our cultural heritage, and supporting each other throughout our academic journey.

Our association serves as a bridge between Indian culture and the international environment of GSMU, organizing cultural events, academic support sessions, and social gatherings that help students feel at home while studying abroad.
